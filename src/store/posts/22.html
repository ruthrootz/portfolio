“Once men turned their thinking over to machines in the hope that this would set them free. But that only permitted other men with machines to enslave them.” - Frank Herbert, Dune

I barely recognize myself. I write way more code, I'm better at editing sheet music, I've made my app ideas come to life, I got promoted to senior software engineer and am in training to be a staff engineer... Overall, I'm a more productive human than I was this time last year, and I've learned more about software development over the last year than over the entire rest of my career.

And I hate it.

I can't remember the last time I felt focused at work. My ability to sit still and read a book has all but disappeared. I feel compelled to always be listening to a podcast. I can sit with a difficult problem for t-minus five minutes, on a good day.

Is this AI's fault? Not completely, no. This attention degradation started years ago. But using Claude at work has kicked the issue into high gear. It used to be that, even if I was distracted in my free time, I at least had focused time during work hours, and that kept me feeling normal. Now the dam has broken, and the distracted feeling is constant. I prompt, get distracted, prompt and get distracted. That's the sum of my work days. But it's not just getting distracted that's the issue. I also feel like my skills are disappearing. Could I even hand-write code anymore? Could I write an essay without constantly flipping to Claude to ask questions? (I'll let you know at the end of this essay.) Could I edit musicXML files by hand? Claude is in its "skills for me but not for thee" era.

Before AI, things used to be difficult. Everyday life was full of challenges. But modern technology is trying its very best to remove all difficulty and all friction. Today, we have to make an effort to make things difficult for ourselves, which literally goes against every fiber of our being. But try I will. I will go for walks without my earbuds. I will read physical books again. I might use Claude to help me edit musicXML files, but it can't play the piano for me.

If work is no longer full of challenges and focus time, my non-work hours will be.
We were made to be efficient, and now I'm asking myself to be inefficient. To embrace the slower way. The harder way. But the alternative is continuing to be more and more productive and feel more and more like a robot every day. And, no thanks, I'll pass.
